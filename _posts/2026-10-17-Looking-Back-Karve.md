# Looking Back

**A must-read for anyone who has lived in Pune or its surroundings.**

*Looking Back* is the autobiography of [Maharshi Dhondo Keshav
Karve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dhondo_Keshav_Karve), the man after whom
Karvenagar is named. It is a short, plain and honest account of a life spent
building things for others.

* **Discover Pune's history:** Understand the roots of SNDT, Fergusson College
  and institutions around Karvenagar, Sinhagad Road and Hingane Budruk. Many of
  us pass these names every day without knowing the story behind them.

* **An ordinary man, extraordinary achievements:** Karve wasn't a Nobel
  laureate or an exceptional genius. His grit and perseverance helped him
  achieve what seemed impossible.

* **Social reform through action:** Widow rehabilitation, widow remarriage,
  girls' education and the Nishkama Karma Math. He married a widow himself when
  doing so meant being cast out by his own community.

* **Building institutions:** He didn't just talk about change. He built
  institutions that continue to serve society.

* **Governance that outlives the founder:** He handed control to life members,
  a Senate and a Syndicate rather than keeping it himself. That is why his
  institutions still thrive a century later.

* **A Satyashodhak spirit:** Question social conventions. Seek truth. Turn
  conviction into action.

* **Remarkable omissions:** Despite covering the period from 1892 to 1930, the
  book makes no reference to the freedom struggle. Religious references are
  also absent, apart from his own atheism.

* **A timeless lesson:** You don't need extraordinary talent or resources to
  change society. Grit, perseverance and a clear purpose can take you a long
  way.

**Read it to understand Karve. Read it to understand Pune. Read it to discover
what one determined individual can accomplish.**
