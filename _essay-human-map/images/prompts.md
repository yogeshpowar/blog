# Cover image prompts

For the cover of "Human Map, Agent Hands". Each concept has a prompt and a
negative prompt. Paste them into any AI image tool.

## Rules for every image (from the brief)

- No real products, brand logos, real data, or identifiable people. Hands
  are fine; faces are not.
- No text in the image. Image tools garble lettering, and the title may
  still change. Put the title in the page, not the picture.
- Calm, not hype: no glowing robot heads, no sci-fi blue brains.
- Size: 1200 × 630 (works as a blog header and as a social preview card).
  In tools that use aspect ratios, ask for 1.91:1, or 16:9 as the next best.
- Check every result for stray logos or lettering before using it.

## Concept 1: The map table (recommended)

The essay's central image, said literally: a person draws the map, and many
small agent hands build inside the lines.

**Prompt**

> Overhead view of a large hand-drawn map on warm paper spread across a
> wooden table. A single human hand holds a pencil, drawing a boundary line
> around regions of the map. The regions are shaded softly in green, amber,
> and muted red, like risk zones. Inside the green region, many small,
> simple, faceless figures made of light grey geometric shapes are busy
> building tiny blocks and paths, staying inside the pencil lines. Fewer
> figures work in the amber region, and the red region is empty except for
> a small flag. Calm, editorial illustration, soft natural light, muted
> colour palette, generous empty space at the top for a headline, no text.

**Negative prompt**

> text, letters, words, numbers, logo, watermark, signature, face, person's
> face, robot head, glowing eyes, sci-fi, neon, circuit brain, clutter,
> brand names

## Concept 2: Blueprint and builders

Closer to software: a person sketches the plan, agents fill it in.

**Prompt**

> A large architectural blueprint on a drafting table, drawn in white lines
> on deep blue paper. On the left half, a human hand with a pencil sketches
> the outline of a building and marks a few areas with small red and amber
> circles. On the right half, the same outline is being filled in with
> small, precise, three-dimensional blocks placed by thin mechanical arms
> that stay within the drawn lines. The two halves blend in the middle.
> Clean editorial illustration, calm and precise, soft shadows, no text.

**Negative prompt**

> text, letters, numbers, labels, logo, watermark, face, humanoid robot,
> glowing eyes, sci-fi city, neon, lens flare, brand names

## Concept 3: The board as terrain

A nod to the author's start in embedded systems: a circuit board seen as a
landscape, with a hand-drawn map laid over it.

**Prompt**

> A green printed circuit board seen from above, its traces and components
> forming a landscape like valleys, roads, and towns. A translucent sheet of
> tracing paper lies over half of it, with a hand-drawn map in pencil:
> boundary lines, a few circled zones, and a dotted route. A human hand
> rests at the edge of the paper holding a pencil. Small points of soft
> light travel along the traces only inside the circled zones. Macro
> photography style, shallow depth of field, warm and cool light balanced,
> no text.

**Negative prompt**

> text, letters, numbers, part numbers, chip markings, logo, brand names,
> watermark, face, robot, sci-fi glow, neon, clutter

## Concept 4: Two halves, one loop (minimal)

The chapter 4 diagram as a picture. Simplest to get right; works small.

**Prompt**

> Minimal flat illustration on an off-white background. On the left, a
> folded paper map in warm tones with a pencil resting on it. On the right,
> a neat grid of small cool-grey cubes being arranged by simple abstract
> hands. Two curved arrows form a loop between the map and the cubes, one
> arrow above and one below. Lots of empty space, soft muted palette,
> calm, modern editorial style, no text.

**Negative prompt**

> text, letters, words, numbers, logo, watermark, face, robot, 3D render
> gloss, neon, gradient background, clutter, brand names
